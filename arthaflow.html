Backup and data storage

Your data is saved only in this browser (localStorage). It isn’t encrypted, synced between devices or backed up, and clearing browser data erases it. Avoid shared computers. Use “Show backup” to generate a copyable text backup, then save it somewhere safe. Backups are manual.
